# Platform and browser icons

`paths.json` contains monochrome SVG paths on a 24 × 24 viewBox, shared by the
NSGo client and the website. Artwork: [Simple Icons v11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons),
[CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/LICENSE.md).
Brand names and logos remain the property of their respective owners.

Keys correspond to apple, windows11, android, linux, googlechrome,
microsoftedge, firefoxbrowser, safari, opera, brave and vivaldi.
